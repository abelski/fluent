| 4 | 2026-08-20 | Fix #158: the instrumental (Įnagininkas) rule card described only declensions I–III, while the exercise pool contains IV (*sūnus*, *profesorius*) and V (*sesuo*, *duktė*, *vanduo*) nouns. | 5 | 2026-08-20 | Review flow rework: maturity-aware stage graph + interleaved scheduling. A **mature** word (`status=known` and `sm2_reps >= MATURE_WORD_REPS`, decided server-side and sent as `mature`) now opens on the typing card with no answer-revealing flashcard; a miss or the new «Забыл» button drops it into the full learning flow (CARD → SELECT → ASSEMBLE → TYPE). «Легко» → type, and a typing miss demotes to the difficult path (SELECT → ASSEMBLE → TYPE) — which now runs for **every** entry type, because `buildAssemblyTiles` (`frontend/lib/assembleTiles.ts`) picks whole-word, syllable or letter tiles instead of `isSingleWordEntry` skipping phrases, multi-form entries and one-syllable words entirely. A mistake anywhere in a chain costs 2× assemble + 2× type, once per word per session (1× + 1× in quick mode). Cards are no longer spliced in as a contiguous block: `scheduleCards` (`frontend/lib/scheduleCards.ts`) interleaves them so no two cards of one word sit next to each other and no three consecutive cards share an exercise bucket — the `'3s'` near-miss drill is the one deliberate exception, keeping its near-the-front placement. Two words with an identical translation can no longer meet: `_dedupe_by_translation` runs in all six session-building endpoints with a `limit × 3` over-fetch so a dropped twin is backfilled, `pickDistractors`/`buildOptions2r` also de-duplicate on the *displayed* translation, and the collision key deliberately **keeps** parentheses so issue #152's qualifiers stay distinguishable. Audit: `backend/scripts/audit_duplicate_translations.py` (52 ru groups / 133 words, 20 same-list). See `documentation/review-flow-stage-graph.md`. |
